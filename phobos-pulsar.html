Phobos Pulsar

Personal emergency alert device for location tracking via GPS and LTE-M.

The Problem

During a personal emergency, I realized that relying on a smartphone for safety is a risk. When the battery is dead, you are cut off from the world. I needed a way to signal for help that is always ready, regardless of mobile apps, WiFi or secondary devices.

The Solution

Phobos Pulsar enables users to transmit their GPS location to a pre-defined contact with a single 3-second button press. By operating independently of WiFi and smartphones via LTE-M connectivity, it serves as a reliable emergency safety net.

Design & Fabrication

Technical Specifications

Component Reference

Enclosure3D Printed PLA+

How it works

  1. 3-second long press on the button.
  2. GPS location acquisition.
  3. Data transmission via LTE-M to a defined contact (Telegram Bot API).
  4. Haptic feedback confirmation.

Future Improvements

  • Custom PCB design for improved reliability and space optimization.
  • Integration of native LilyGo buttons for reduced device volume.
  • Exploration of waterproof housing for outdoor usage.
  • Battery level indication system.
