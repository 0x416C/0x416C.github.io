Phobos Pulsar

Personal emergency alert device for location tracking via LTE-M.

The Problem

This project was born from a real-world emergency: a crisis situation where a lack of communication (dead smartphone battery) led to a critical information vacuum, leaving an individual isolated during a medical emergency. This highlighted the urgent need for a dedicated, autonomous safety device that bypasses consumer mobile limitations.

The Solution

Phobos Pulsar is a compact, autonomous hardware solution designed to provide immediate assistance. It enables users to transmit their GPS location to a pre-defined contact with a single 3-second button press. By operating independently of WiFi and smartphones via LTE-M connectivity, it serves as a reliable emergency safety net.

How it works

  • 3-second long press on the button.
  • GPS location acquisition.
  • Data transmission via LTE-M to a defined contact (Telegram Bot API).
  • Haptic feedback confirmation.

Future Improvements

  • Custom PCB design for improved reliability and space optimization.
  • Integration of native LilyGo buttons for reduced device volume.
  • Exploration of waterproof housing for outdoor usage.
  • Battery level indication system.
