Phobos Pulsar

Personal emergency alert device for location tracking via LTE-M.

The Problem

Developed following an emergency situation where a lack of communication during a crisis highlighted the need for a reliable, autonomous safety device[cite: 1].

The Solution

A compact, standalone device allowing emergency location transmission via a single 3-second button press, independent of WiFi or smartphones[cite: 1].

Technical Specifications

ComponentReference
Main BoardLilyGo T-SIM7080G (LTE-M/GPS)
PowerLiPo 3.7V 500mAh
FeedbackHaptic vibration motor
Enclosure3D Printed PLA+

How it works

  • 3-second long press on the button[cite: 1].
  • GPS location acquisition[cite: 1].
  • Data transmission via LTE-M to a defined contact (Telegram Bot API)[cite: 1].
  • Haptic feedback confirmation[cite: 1].

Future Improvements

  • Custom PCB design for improved reliability and space optimization[cite: 1].
  • Integration of native LilyGo buttons for reduced device volume[cite: 1].
  • Exploration of waterproof housing for outdoor usage[cite: 1].
  • Battery level indication system[cite: 1].
